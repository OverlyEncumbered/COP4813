Which would I choose?

For a general website, I would start with WordPress because its themes and plugins cover many common needs. Drupal would be worth considering for more structured content and complex permissions. Joomla is another option for multilingual sites with different user access levels. Ghost fits a publication built around newsletters and subscriptions, while Grav fits a smaller content site where file-based storage is useful.

These are my selection judgments based on the capabilities and tradeoffs documented in the table.
