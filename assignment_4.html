RC Circuit Charging Calculator

This calculator finds the voltage across a charging capacitor over time using the equation below.

V(t) = Vs(1 - e-t/(RC))

V(t) is the capacitor voltage, Vs is the source voltage, R is resistance, C is capacitance, and t is time.
